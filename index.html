HOW THE SIMULATOR WORKS

Personal Loot Simulator Only: This tool simulates personal purples received for your own chest rolls—it does not track or care about total "purples seen" across a raid team. Team size, contribution %, and deaths only adjust your personal roll rate.
  • Rolling Loot: Use Roll Loot in Single, Batch, or Auto mode to simulate completions. Non-purple raids roll standard loot along with any eligible tertiary drops.
  • Unique & Pet Reveals: Rolling a personal purple or first-time pet pauses automatic/batch runs and opens the reveal modal so you can claim your reward.
  • Tracking & Export: Your collection log, session loot totals, estimated GP values, and CSV export update automatically in your local browser session.
